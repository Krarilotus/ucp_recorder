# UCP Recorder

Record single-player Skirmishes and multiplayer matches automatically, and watch them locally through single-player battle history. Save a named snapshot from the pause menu; the full recording continues in the background.

Experimental build for Crusader and Extreme. Playback requires the recorded UCP settings and exact extension versions. Multiplayer recovery and broader live testing remain.
